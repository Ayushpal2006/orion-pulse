**App name (≤30):** Apka Bill: GST Billing & Stock

**Short description (≤80):** GST billing, stock and customer dues for Indian shops. Works offline.

**Full description (≤4000):**

Apka Bill is a billing app for Indian shops: kirana and grocery stores, general stores, mobile and electronics shops, clothing stores, restaurants and cafes.

Make the bill in seconds
• Search by name, SKU or barcode, change quantity, add discount and GST.
• Take Cash, UPI, Card or Due/Credit. Partial payments keep the balance as due.
• Print on 58mm Bluetooth thermal printers, with your logo, UPI QR and barcode, each one switchable.
• Share the bill with the customer on WhatsApp.

Keep billing without internet
• Bills are saved on the phone when the network drops and sync when you are back online.

Know your shop
• Stock updates with every sale and purchase; low and out-of-stock items stand out.
• Customer purchases and dues (khata).
• Suppliers, purchases and supplier balances.
• Daily sales, profit and expenses, with PDF and Excel export.

More than one shop
• Run several stores under one account; each user sees only the stores they are allowed to.

Restaurants and cafes
• Send kitchen items to a separate KOT printer.

Your shop's data is kept separate from every other shop and travels only over encrypted connections.
