Last updated: [date]

Apka Bill ("we") provides billing and stock software for shops. This policy explains what data the Apka Bill app and website handle.

## What we collect
- **Account details** of shop owners and staff: name, email, phone number, and a password (stored as a one-way hash).
- **Business records the shop enters**: products, prices, stock, sales, purchases, expenses, suppliers, and the shop's customers (name, phone, email, address, purchases and dues). The shop decides what to enter. We process this data on the shop's behalf.
- **Images the shop uploads**, such as a logo or product photos.

We do **not** collect location, contacts, advertising IDs, or app-usage analytics, and the app contains no advertising or analytics SDKs. The Bluetooth and (on Android 11 and older) location permissions exist only so the app can find Bluetooth receipt printers; the app never reads your location.

## How we use it
Only to run the service: billing, stock, reports, syncing between devices, printing and sharing bills that the shop chooses to share.

## Where it is stored and who processes it
Data is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS) and stored with our service providers: Cloudflare (application hosting) and Neon (database). If a shop turns on optional features, Google (Google Sheets sync) or Cloudinary (image hosting) also process the relevant data. We do not sell personal data or share it for advertising.

Bills shared over WhatsApp are sent by the shop through WhatsApp, under WhatsApp's own terms.

## Separation between shops
Each shop's data is kept separate. Staff see only the stores their account is allowed to access.

## Retention and deletion
We keep data while the shop's account is active. To delete an account and its data, contact [support email]. We will delete it within [30] days, except where the law requires us to keep records.

## Children
Apka Bill is a business tool and is not directed at children under 13.
